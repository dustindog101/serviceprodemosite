## Service pages
**Water damage:** flood cleanup, water extraction, moisture detection (moisture meters, infrared cameras), drying, antimicrobial treatment, mold remediation; 24/7 extraction with industrial pumps and drying equipment. FAQs:
1. How soon to act? Immediately; first 24-48 hours are critical to prevent mold.
2. Insurance? We work directly with insurers and handle the claim.
3. Damage assessment? Moisture meters and infrared cameras find hidden water.
4. Mold? Standing water/excess moisture can cause mold; inspection and remediation included.
5. Prevention? Guidance on fixing leaks, drainage, water-detection devices.
6. Emergency extraction? 24/7, industrial pumps and drying equipment.
7. Residential or commercial? Both.

**Mold remediation:** process = evaluate visible mold and moisture, contain affected area, remove materials that can't be cleaned, clean surfaces, fix moisture source, dry, repair. FAQs:
1. How quickly can mold spread? Can begin on damp surfaces within 24-48 hours and spread within days.
2. Health risks? Breathing issues, allergic reactions, headaches, skin irritation; higher risk with allergies, asthma, weak immune systems.
3. Remove it myself? Small areas under about 10 sq ft may be manageable; professional removal recommended for larger/unclear areas.

**Fire damage:** FAQs:
1. When to call? As soon as the fire department confirms it is safe to enter.
2. Insurance? We work directly with insurers.
3. Assessment? Full inspection of structure, soot, heat, smoke and water damage, documented.
4. Mold risk? Firefighting water can cause mold; moisture inspection, extraction, drying included.
5. While waiting? Stay out of damaged areas, don't disturb soot, shut off utilities only if professionals instruct.
6. Timeline? Minor smoke damage: days; structural: weeks or longer; estimate at inspection.
7. Emergency response? 24/7: board-up, stabilization, debris removal, smoke containment.
8. Safety? Protective gear; assessment of structure, electrical risks, air quality, contamination.

**Remodeling:** kitchens, bathrooms, basements, structural repairs, flooring, cabinetry, paint and drywall, post-disaster reconstruction. FAQs:
1. Services offered? Remodeling types above plus restoration of water/fire/mold-affected spaces.
2. Timeline? Depends on size, scope, condition; consultation gives estimate.
3. Insurance work? We work directly with insurers on covered losses and coordinate paperwork.
